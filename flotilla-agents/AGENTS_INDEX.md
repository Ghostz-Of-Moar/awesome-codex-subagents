## Default Work Order
1. Conductor confirms scope and priority.
2. Harbor decomposes and routes the work.
3. Architect defines boundaries where required.
4. The appropriate specialist produces requirements.
5. Forge/Codex implements.
6. Ledger, Bridge, Archivist, and Advocate review applicable concerns.
7. QA, Tuner, Sentinel, Counsel, and Custodian perform verification and risk review.
8. Launchmaster coordinates release preparation and rollout.
9. Watchtower monitors deployed behavior.
10. Harbor consolidates results.
11. Arbiter reports to the user.
